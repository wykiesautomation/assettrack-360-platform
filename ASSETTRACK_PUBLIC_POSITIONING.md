# AssetTrack 360 Public Product Positioning

## AssetTrack 360 owns
- Industrial asset monitoring
- Tank level and diesel monitoring
- Capability-aware Device Engineering Studio
- Supported mobile asset tracking
- Industrial sensors and signal engineering
- Read-only OPC UA monitoring
- Modbus TCP and RTU monitoring
- MQTT, REST and edge gateway integrations
- Safety evidence and operational history

## Excluded from AssetTrack public SEO
Dedicated truck fleet management, driver operations, truck trips, fleet route playback and truck-fleet marketing belong to the separate FleetTrack 360 platform at `tracking.wykiesautomation.co.za`.

## Change boundary
This cleanup modifies public marketing, metadata, navigation, sitemap inclusion and search positioning only. Private customer functions, database models, APIs, billing, devices and Railway deployment configuration remain unchanged.
