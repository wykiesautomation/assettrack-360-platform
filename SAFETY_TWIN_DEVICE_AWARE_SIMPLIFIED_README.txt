AssetTrack 360 Device-Aware Safety Twin Simplification

Safety Twin is now a focused safety status and evidence view, not a second application inside AssetTrack 360.
Removed: NOW/PAST/NEXT controls, Counterfactual Safety Lab, duplicated history and prediction surfaces.
Preserved as separate workflows: Tracking History, Geofences & Rules, Evidence Centre and Motion Setup.
Android native trackers show motion capabilities only when the registered phone reported the required sensors.
SIM808 shows GPS Truth, Geofence Monitoring, Unexpected Movement and Tracker Health only.
Unsupported capabilities are hidden rather than presented as misleading controls.
