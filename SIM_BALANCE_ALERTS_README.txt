AssetTrack 360 SIM Balance Alerts

Airtime: LOW below R20, CRITICAL below R10, critical recovery above R15.
Data: LOW below 250 MB, CRITICAL below 100 MB.
Notifications: in-app Alarm plus email queue, 24-hour cooldown.
Unknown or unparsed USSD responses are not stored as zero and do not create false alarms.
Firmware checks balances every 6 hours and sends only valid parsed values.
